01 Mission

Why Allotar exists

To turn protein dynamics into selective, validated therapeutic opportunities.

Allotar is building a physics-driven platform for allosteric drug discovery—revealing hidden regulatory sites, designing selective interventions, and improving every cycle through functional validation at scale.

  • Translate conformational ensembles into actionable, druggable insights
  • Prioritize regulatory allosteric sites with selectivity in mind
  • Design across modalities and learn from real experimental outcomes
